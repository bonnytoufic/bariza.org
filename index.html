Who We Are

Bariza Pulmonary Support Services LLC is a Southern California respiratory consulting firm founded by Bonny Toufic, BSRC, BBA, RRT, RCP. We exist to bridge the gap between clinical best practices and real-world respiratory care delivery.

  • Clinical consulting for acute & post-acute care
  • Evidence-based staff & patient education
  • Pulmonary program development & quality improvement

Our mission is to improve respiratory outcomes through education, consulting, and partnership.

— Bonny Toufic, RRT, RCP
