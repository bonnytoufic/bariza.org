Who We Are

Bariza Pulmonary Support Services LLC is a Southern California respiratory consulting firm founded by Bonny Toufic, BBA, BSRC, RRT, RCP. We specialize in bringing expert respiratory care directly to patients in their homes.

  • In-home respiratory assessments & education
  • Home oxygen therapy & equipment training
  • CPAP/BiPAP setup & patient coaching
  • Caregiver training & family support

Our mission is to improve respiratory outcomes through education, consulting, and partnership.

— Bonny Toufic, RRT, RCP

Home Respiratory Services

Comprehensive respiratory support for home health agencies, patients, and families.

In-Home Respiratory Care

Professional respiratory assessments, education, and support delivered directly to patients in their homes.

Oxygen Therapy Support

Home oxygen setup, education, and monitoring for patients requiring supplemental oxygen therapy.

CPAP/BiPAP Coaching

Device setup, mask fitting, and patient education to ensure successful sleep apnea therapy at home.

Patient & Family Education

COPD management, medication education, and caregiver training to support long-term respiratory health.

Home Equipment Solutions

We provide education, setup support, and clinical guidance for respiratory equipment used in the home setting.

  • Home oxygen concentrators & portable systems
  • CPAP, BiPAP & non-invasive ventilation
  • Nebulizers & aerosol therapy devices
  • Equipment setup & safety training

We partner with DME providers to ensure seamless equipment delivery and support.

Home Care Excellence

We help patients and families transition to home respiratory care with confidence, reducing hospital readmissions and improving quality of life.
